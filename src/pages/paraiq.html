ParaIQ runs within a designated local application environment, where client cases, working documents and drafts are maintained.

For AI-assisted processing, relevant information is transmitted to the configured external AI service provider. Our implementation is designed to use providers with zero-data-retention arrangements.

This architecture allows us to integrate AI capabilities into paraplanning workflows while maintaining clear distinctions between local document handling and external AI processing.
