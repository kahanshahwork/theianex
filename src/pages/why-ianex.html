Delivering good advice shouldn't feel like climbing a mountain.

As a financial adviser, your work involves much more than meeting clients and making recommendations.

Behind every piece of advice are client documents to review, information to consolidate, gaps to resolve, strategies to prepare and documents to check.

These processes can consume a considerable amount of time when handled manually.

We understand the work involved. Theianex combines professional paraplanning with purpose-built technology to make the process more organised and manageable.

Bringing information together shouldn't take the time it does.

Client information often arrives across multiple fact-finds, statements, emails and supporting documents. Reviewing each file and manually consolidating the relevant details adds considerable work before advice preparation even begins.

How Theianex helps

ParaIQ processes supported client documents together, extracts relevant information and organises it into a structured format. Our paraplanners can work from this organised information rather than repeatedly entering the same details.

Identify information gaps before they become delays.

A missing balance, outdated statement or conflicting client detail can interrupt the preparation process and lead to repeated follow-ups.

How Theianex helps

With ParaIQ, our team can process the available documents and perform an initial review, highlighting potential gaps and inconsistencies for examination. This helps consolidate queries earlier in the workflow.

Less time spent on repetitive document work.

Preparing advice documentation involves carrying information across multiple sections, checking figures and ensuring the document reflects the adviser's instructions.

How Theianex helps

ParaIQ assists with structured information and SOA drafting. Our paraplanners then examine, refine and review the prepared document before returning it to the adviser.

Your workload changes. Your support should accommodate it.

Advice requests, review deadlines and client requirements do not always arrive evenly. This can put additional pressure on an internal paraplanning team.

How Theianex helps

We provide additional paraplanning capacity according to your agreed service requirements, scope and availability.

Consistency needs attention at every stage.

Client information, assumptions and supporting details need to remain consistent throughout the advice documentation. A structured review process is essential to identify discrepancies before the document is returned.

How Theianex helps

ParaIQ assists in identifying potential inconsistencies, while our paraplanners examine the prepared work and address relevant issues before handover.
