Questions

Outsourced paraplanning, answered plainly.

What is paraplanning?

Paraplanning is the preparation work behind financial advice: reviewing client information, researching products and strategies, and drafting advice documents such as a Statement of Advice (SOA) or Record of Advice (ROA). The adviser decides the recommendations. The paraplanner prepares the document and checks it.

What does Theianex prepare?

SOAs, ROAs and supporting advice documents. Client file preparation, including fact-find reviews, client summaries and file notes. Assigned investment and product research, and strategy documentation. And document review for consistency, completeness and alignment with your instructions.

How does outsourced paraplanning work with our practice?

We confirm the scope and deliverables first. You send the client documents and adviser instructions. We process and review them, prepare and check the advice document, and return it with a handover note listing outstanding matters. What to ask before you outsource paraplanning

Who is responsible for the advice?

The adviser. We prepare and check the document, and the adviser reviews the recommendations and retains responsibility for final approval and sign-off. Theianex does not provide personal advice to your clients.

How does technology fit into the work?

Our team uses ParaIQ to process client documents, extract information into a structured file and flag potential gaps. Paraplanners then review the findings, carry out the assigned research and preparation, and check the work before it is returned. Can AI write a Statement of Advice?

Will you work to our templates and processes?

That is the aim. Every practice has its own templates, document requirements and internal processes, so before work starts we agree the scope, service expectations and how documents and questions will be passed back and forth, then refine through your feedback.
