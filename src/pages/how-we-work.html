Who does what

One document. Three roles.

Technology does the groundwork.

Reads the client file, pulls out the facts, drafts, and checks the draft against the file.

Paraplanners do the thinking.

Analysis, review and diligence on every document. Every flag the technology raises is reviewed by a person.

You sign off.

Advice decisions stay with the adviser.
